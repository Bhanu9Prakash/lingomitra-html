# Konkani: A pattern-first foundation

This course teaches standard Goan Konkani in Devanagari, broadly the Antruz-based written standard, using a small neutral conversational register. Konkani also has established Roman, Kannada, Malayalam and Perso-Arabic writing traditions and substantial regional and community variation. This romanisation is only an access aid, not Romi orthography or a claim that the scripts encode identical varieties. It uses ā ī ū, nasal vowels with a tilde, ṭ ḍ ṇ ḷ for retroflex sounds, and sh represented by ś. Written a can have a central or o-like quality in speech. Adult feminine reference is labelled because social and dialectal agreement can differ.

## Lesson 1: Names before an alphabet lecture

**Outcome:** Give a name and ask a familiar person theirs.

### Think before reading

If म्हजें नांव means my name, can putting a name after it already make a useful introduction?

### Build the pattern

म्हजें नांव नीला gives the useful introduction my name is Nila. Konkani can use a verbless identity expression in such an introduction. You do not need to insert Marathi आहे or Hindi है. The next lesson introduces Konkani’s own present existence forms where they are useful.

नांव means name and is neuter. म्हजें, my, is the neuter form that belongs with it. तुजें gives familiar your in तुजें नांव कितें?, what is your name? तुमचें gives respectful or plural your. Respect and noun agreement are two independent choices: the addressee determines the possessive family, and name determines its neuter ending.

The course uses standard Goan Konkani in Devanagari, broadly the written Antruz-based standard. It does not combine Mangalorean, Saraswat and Roman-script Goan paradigms into one invented uniform dialect. Real speakers use several legitimate varieties, and a different form in another community is not automatically incorrect.

The romanisation marks nasal vowels with a tilde, as nā̃v and mhajẽ. This is a learning notation, not standard Romi orthography. The nasal mark is not an instruction to pronounce a final English m. Begin by recognising the complete name phrases, then notice the repeated noun and the changing possessive. A small successful introduction provides a reason to learn the script rather than a reward postponed until every letter is memorised.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| म्हजें नांव नीला. | Mhajẽ nā̃v Nīlā. | My name is Nila. |
| तुजें नांव कितें? | Tujẽ nā̃v kitẽ? | What is your name, familiarly? |
| तुमचें नांव कितें? | Tumchẽ nā̃v kitẽ? | What is your name, respectfully? |
| म्हजें नांव राजू. | Mhajẽ nā̃v Rājū. | My name is Raju. |
| नमस्कार. | Namaskār. | Hello, a respectful greeting. |
| हांव नीला. | Hā̃v Nīlā. | I am Nila. |

### Common trap

Do not insert a neighbouring language’s copula simply because English has is. Also, the romanisation is not a claim that Romi Konkani is merely Devanagari converted letter by letter; it is a separate written tradition.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Introduce yourself by the name Nila, using my name.
2. Ask a close friend their name.
3. Ask an adult respectfully for their name.
4. Introduce yourself by the name Raju, using my name.

***Answers:***

1. `म्हजें नांव नीला.` - `Mhajẽ nā̃v Nīlā.`
2. `तुजें नांव कितें?` - `Tujẽ nā̃v kitẽ?`
3. `तुमचें नांव कितें?` - `Tumchẽ nā̃v kitẽ?`
4. `म्हजें नांव राजू.` - `Mhajẽ nā̃v Rājū.`

### Why these answers work

The introductions keep म्हजें नांव and replace only the personal name. Both questions use कितें for the unknown name. तुजें versus तुमचें changes the address relationship, while the neuter ending remains because नांव stays the same noun.

### Retrieve and recombine

Introduce two imaginary people by speaking as each in turn. Then ask one name respectfully. Retrieve the nasal endings without treating the printed tilde as a separate consonant.


## Lesson 2: Here, there and being present

**Outcome:** Locate yourself and another person with Konkani forms of be.

### Think before reading

If हांगा means here, what does adding आसा tell you beyond merely naming a place?

### Build the pattern

आसा states present existence or location with a singular third-person referent. तो हांगा आसा means he is here; ती थंय आसा means she is there. The same आसा works with male and female singular subjects in these examples. It does not copy their gender into separate endings.

First-person हांव takes आसां in the written pattern taught here: हांव हांगा आसां. The nasal ending distinguishes it from third-person आसा. Plural and respectful forms will appear next. Some colloquial materials represent or pronounce these forms differently; the course keeps the selected written standard consistent.

हांगा means here and थंय means there. Neither requires an additional word corresponding to English at in these simple location sentences. They are adverbs, so do not assume that every place noun will behave the same way. A house or a school will later take its own location form.

The object or person being located normally comes before the location expression and final verb. In conversation the order can shift with emphasis, but this neutral pattern is an effective starting point. Compare a name introduction with a location statement: the first identifies a person, while the second places them somewhere. Both are useful immediately, yet they need not have identical grammatical packaging. Learn what the predicate contributes before deciding whether a visible copula is required.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| हांव हांगा आसां. | Hā̃v hā̃gā āsā̃. | I am here. |
| तो हांगा आसा. | To hā̃gā āsā. | He is here. |
| ती थंय आसा. | Tī thãy āsā. | She is there. |
| पुस्तक हांगा आसा. | Pustak hā̃gā āsā. | The book is here. |
| घर थंय आसा. | Ghar thãy āsā. | The house is there. |
| तो थंय आसा. | To thãy āsā. | He is there. |

### Common trap

Do not transfer Marathi आहे into Konkani. Also, the first-person nasal आसां and third-person आसा are not randomly interchangeable spellings in the standard used for these lessons.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Say you are here.
2. Say he is here.
3. Say she is there.
4. Say the book is here.

***Answers:***

1. `हांव हांगा आसां.` - `Hā̃v hā̃gā āsā̃.`
2. `तो हांगा आसा.` - `To hā̃gā āsā.`
3. `ती थंय आसा.` - `Tī thãy āsā.`
4. `पुस्तक हांगा आसा.` - `Pustak hā̃gā āsā.`

### Why these answers work

Only the first answer needs the first-person form आसां. The others use singular आसा regardless of the person’s gender or the book’s neuter gender. हांगा and थंय supply the location rather than changing the identity of the subject.

### Retrieve and recombine

Imagine waiting with a book while a friend stands farther away. Locate yourself, the book and the friend. Change here to there once without changing who is being located.


## Lesson 3: Respectful address and plural presence

**Outcome:** Address someone politely and locate a group.

### Think before reading

One adult is being addressed respectfully. Must a plural-looking verb mean there are physically several listeners?

### Build the pattern

तूं is familiar singular you. तुमी is plural you and also serves respectful address. In the present location construction, तुमी pairs with आसात. A respectful singular addressee therefore uses the same form as several listeners in this pattern. The social meaning cannot be determined by counting people alone.

आमी means we and also takes आसात here. This course does not impose Marathi’s or Gujarati’s inclusive-exclusive pronoun lesson on Konkani: pronoun systems differ by language and variety. For the selected elementary Goan pattern, आमी is the useful first-person plural form being practised.

ते is a masculine plural reference, while तीं can refer to neuter plural things. The masculine plural ते हांगा आसात means they are here. Neuter plural objects will receive fuller attention with plural nouns later. For now, the examples let you hear the shared plural existence form.

Respectful grammar is one part of courtesy. A blunt question can remain socially awkward despite तुमी, while a gentle request can be appropriate in a familiar relationship. Choose the address form according to the people involved and the setting. When unsure with an adult stranger, the respectful form is a useful starting point. Do not switch back to तूं inside the same exchange merely because a shorter sentence feels easier to remember.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| तुमी हांगा आसात. | Tumī hā̃gā āsāt. | You are here, respectfully or plurally. |
| तूं हांगा आसा. | Tū̃ hā̃gā āsā. | You are here, familiarly. |
| आमी थंय आसात. | Āmī thãy āsāt. | We are there. |
| ते हांगा आसात. | Te hā̃gā āsāt. | They are here, masculine plural. |
| तुमी थंय आसात. | Tumī thãy āsāt. | You are there, respectfully or plurally. |
| आमी हांगा आसात. | Āmī hā̃gā āsāt. | We are here. |

### Common trap

Do not interpret every तुमी sentence as addressing several people. Respectful singular is possible. Also, familiar तूं and respectful तुमी belong to different address patterns and should not be mixed casually inside one exchange.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Say an adult listener is here, respectfully.
2. Say a close friend is here, familiarly.
3. Say we are there.
4. Say they are here, referring to a male group.

***Answers:***

1. `तुमी हांगा आसात.` - `Tumī hā̃gā āsāt.`
2. `तूं हांगा आसा.` - `Tū̃ hā̃gā āsā.`
3. `आमी थंय आसात.` - `Āmī thãy āsāt.`
4. `ते हांगा आसात.` - `Te hā̃gā āsāt.`

### Why these answers work

The familiar singular answer uses आसा, while respectful तुमी, plural आमी and masculine plural ते use आसात. The place adverbs remain unchanged. The first answer can address one adult because grammatical plurality also performs a respect function.

### Retrieve and recombine

Picture a teacher addressing one visitor and then a group. Use the respectful sentence in both situations. Explain why the same form does not force the same number of physical listeners.


## Lesson 4: Pointing and three noun genders

**Outcome:** Identify a nearby boy, woman or house with the correct demonstrative.

### Think before reading

Would the word this necessarily remain the same before a masculine boy and a neuter house?

### Build the pattern

Konkani nouns have masculine, feminine and neuter genders. For nearby singular reference, हो is masculine, ही feminine and हें neuter. Thus हो भुरगो means this boy and हें घर means this house. Gender belongs to the noun; it is not inferred from the owner’s identity.

भुरगो is a masculine boy or male child, बायल a woman and घर a neuter house. Some person words and their agreement are socially and dialectally complex, especially reference to children and women. The course uses explicitly labelled adult or noun-gender examples and avoids claiming that biological sex determines every Konkani agreement choice.

A description can add आसा: हें घर व्हडलें आसा, this house is big. व्हडलें is the neuter form of a changing adjective. बरो, बरी and बरें provide another useful masculine, feminine and neuter set meaning good or well according to context. They describe the noun or person, not the speaker’s attitude toward grammatical gender.

The nasal vowels in हें and बरें must remain visible in reading. A loss of nasalisation can hide a meaningful form difference. The romanisation helps you notice it, but a fluent speaker’s audio is needed for exact vowel quality. Begin with the complete noun phrase, then add the description or location. This keeps agreement anchored to the noun that actually controls it.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| हें घर व्हडलें आसा. | Hẽ ghar vhaḍlẽ āsā. | This house is big. |
| हो भुरगो बरो आसा. | Ho bhurgo baro āsā. | This boy is well. |
| ही बायल बरी आसा. | Hī bāyl barī āsā. | This woman is well. |
| हें पुस्तक बरें आसा. | Hẽ pustak barẽ āsā. | This book is good. |
| हो रस्तो. | Ho rasto. | This road, masculine. |
| ही बाटली. | Hī bāṭlī. | This bottle, feminine. |

### Common trap

Do not use a single this form before every noun. Also, Konkani’s gender system should not be reduced to a universal rule about a person’s sex; keep the explicitly taught noun categories and review local usage.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Say this house is big.
2. Say this boy is well.
3. Say this woman is well.
4. Say this book is good.

***Answers:***

1. `हें घर व्हडलें आसा.` - `Hẽ ghar vhaḍlẽ āsā.`
2. `हो भुरगो बरो आसा.` - `Ho bhurgo baro āsā.`
3. `ही बायल बरी आसा.` - `Hī bāyl barī āsā.`
4. `हें पुस्तक बरें आसा.` - `Hẽ pustak barẽ āsā.`

### Why these answers work

हें pairs with neuter घर and पुस्तक; हो with masculine भुरगो; ही with feminine बायल. The adjectives change accordingly, while the singular copula आसा remains fixed. Each demonstrative belongs with its noun before the full sentence is built.

### Retrieve and recombine

Point mentally to a road, a bottle and a book. Select the demonstrative before adding any description. Then change the house’s location without changing its grammatical gender.


## Lesson 5: What, who and where

**Outcome:** Ask a clear question for missing information.

### Think before reading

If हांगा supplies a known location, what should replace it when the location is unknown?

### Build the pattern

खंय asks where in the Goan forms used here. तो खंय आसा? replaces the known place in a location statement with an information gap. कितें asks what, and कोण asks who. The unknown information can remain before the final predicate rather than moving into English question order.

हें कितें? is a natural short identification question, what is this? A copula can also appear in fuller contexts, but the brief expression is useful when pointing to an unfamiliar object. It does not need an imported word for is. Your earlier name question also used कितें because a name was the missing information.

तुमी खंय आसात? asks respectfully where the listener is. The question word does not erase the respectful plural-form copula. Keep social agreement intact even when the sentence contains unfamiliar question vocabulary.

Yes-or-no questions can be indicated by intonation and context: तो हांगा आसा? checks a complete proposition. A where question instead requests a location that the speaker does not know. These are different listener tasks. A yes or no does not normally answer खंय by itself. When practising, imagine what kind of reply would actually resolve the question. This checks comprehension more effectively than merely recognising a question mark at the end.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| तो खंय आसा? | To khãy āsā? | Where is he? |
| हें कितें? | Hẽ kitẽ? | What is this? |
| कोण हांगा आसा? | Koṇ hā̃gā āsā? | Who is here? |
| तुमी खंय आसात? | Tumī khãy āsāt? | Where are you, respectfully? |
| तो हांगा आसा? | To hā̃gā āsā? | Is he here? |
| पुस्तक खंय आसा? | Pustak khãy āsā? | Where is the book? |

### Common trap

Do not answer where with a bare yes simply because the sentence ends in a question mark. Also, preserve आसात when addressing someone respectfully; a question word does not make the address familiar.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Ask where he is.
2. Ask what this is, using the short expression.
3. Ask who is here.
4. Ask an adult where they are, respectfully.

***Answers:***

1. `तो खंय आसा?` - `To khãy āsā?`
2. `हें कितें?` - `Hẽ kitẽ?`
3. `कोण हांगा आसा?` - `Koṇ hā̃gā āsā?`
4. `तुमी खंय आसात?` - `Tumī khãy āsāt?`

### Why these answers work

खंय asks for a place, कितें for an identity and कोण for a person. The fourth answer retains respectful आसात. The short second answer is a complete conversational question even without a visible present copula.

### Retrieve and recombine

Imagine searching for a book while hearing a voice nearby. Ask where the book is and who is here. Explain which answer category would resolve each question.


## Lesson 6: Present absence with ना

**Outcome:** Deny presence or availability without copying an affirmative copula.

### Think before reading

If आसा means is present, could ना provide a complete negative instead of following an unchanged आसा?

### Build the pattern

ना expresses present absence in the basic existence and location patterns here. पुस्तक हांगा ना means the book is not here. The negative replaces the affirmative आसा rather than being appended after it. A person can likewise be absent: तो हांगा ना.

This short negative should not be treated as the complete negative system for every Konkani verb. Actions such as eating or working have their own negative forms, which appear later. A foundation becomes more accurate when it distinguishes absence, a negative habit and a command not to do something.

The order still identifies the thing or person, the location and the negative predicate. Changing हांगा to थंय changes which place is denied. It does not assert the actual location unless you add a separate positive statement. The book is not here leaves open many possible places where it might be.

A useful search exchange therefore has three stages: ask where, deny an incorrect guess, then supply a known location if you have one. You can do all three with the vocabulary already learned. Avoid treating a negative answer as unhelpful by definition; it narrows the possibilities, but it does not magically supply the missing fact. The practice makes those information limits visible through simple, reusable sentences.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| पुस्तक हांगा ना. | Pustak hā̃gā nā. | The book is not here. |
| तो हांगा ना. | To hā̃gā nā. | He is not here. |
| ती थंय ना. | Tī thãy nā. | She is not there. |
| घर हांगा ना. | Ghar hā̃gā nā. | The house is not here. |
| पुस्तक थंय आसा. | Pustak thãy āsā. | The book is there. |
| उदक ना. | Udak nā. | There is no water. |

### Common trap

Do not combine an unchanged positive आसा with ना in the simple absence pattern. Also, not here does not itself mean there; a negative location leaves the actual place unspecified.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Say the book is not here.
2. Say he is not here.
3. Say she is not there.
4. Say the house is not here.

***Answers:***

1. `पुस्तक हांगा ना.` - `Pustak hā̃gā nā.`
2. `तो हांगा ना.` - `To hā̃gā nā.`
3. `ती थंय ना.` - `Tī thãy nā.`
4. `घर हांगा ना.` - `Ghar hā̃gā nā.`

### Why these answers work

All four use ना as the negative existence or location predicate. The location remains before it. The gender of the person or thing does not produce a separate negative form in these examples, and no affirmative copula survives.

### Retrieve and recombine

Ask for the book’s location, reject here, then place it there with a separate positive sentence. Explain which statement removes a possibility and which supplies the answer.


## Lesson 7: Needs expressed through म्हाका

**Outcome:** Ask for a wanted item without making English I the grammatical starting point.

### Think before reading

If a need is relevant to me, might Konkani use म्हाका rather than ordinary हांव?

### Build the pattern

म्हाका उदक जाय means I want or need water. म्हाका is a to-me form, and जाय supplies the want or need meaning. The ordinary subject pronoun हांव is not substituted mechanically in this construction. The desired item comes between the experiencer and जाय.

तुका is familiar to you and तुमकां respectful or plural to you. तुमकां कितें जाय? asks what an adult wants or needs. The polite address distinction therefore appears in more than one pronoun family: तूं versus तुमी as subjects, and तुका versus तुमकां in this experiencer role.

उदक is the selected Goan word for water; दूद means milk. Other Konkani communities may use different familiar vocabulary, but the course keeps one set consistent. पुस्तक and मदत let the same construction request a concrete object or abstract help. A need is not restricted to a thing that can physically be held.

A statement of need is not necessarily a direct command to the listener. It can explain your situation, after which you ask for a specific action. This distinction matters in a shop, a classroom or a visit. Decide first what you need, then whether the person can reasonably provide it. The next lesson adds respectful commands so that the action request can be made explicitly.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| म्हाका उदक जाय. | Mhākā udak jāy. | I want water. |
| म्हाका मदत जाय. | Mhākā madat jāy. | I need help. |
| तुमकां कितें जाय? | Tumkā̃ kitẽ jāy? | What do you want, respectfully? |
| म्हाका पुस्तक जाय. | Mhākā pustak jāy. | I want a book. |
| तुका दूद जाय? | Tukā dūd jāy? | Do you want milk, familiarly? |
| म्हाका दूद जाय. | Mhākā dūd jāy. | I want milk. |

### Common trap

Do not replace म्हाका with हांव just because English says I want. Also, a respectful subject तुमी does not remain unchanged in the to-you role: use तुमकां for the pattern taught here.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Say you want water.
2. Say you need help.
3. Ask an adult what they want.
4. Say you want a book.

***Answers:***

1. `म्हाका उदक जाय.` - `Mhākā udak jāy.`
2. `म्हाका मदत जाय.` - `Mhākā madat jāy.`
3. `तुमकां कितें जाय?` - `Tumkā̃ kitẽ jāy?`
4. `म्हाका पुस्तक जाय.` - `Mhākā pustak jāy.`

### Why these answers work

म्हाका identifies the experiencer in the three statements. The question uses respectful तुमकां and replaces the desired item with कितें. जाय stays fixed in this elementary pattern, so the object can change without importing Marathi हवा agreement.

### Retrieve and recombine

Imagine needing water and help finding a room. State each need separately, then ask someone respectfully what they need. Keep the experiencer form distinct from an ordinary subject.


## Lesson 8: Respectful commands in a real exchange

**Outcome:** Ask someone to sit, give, come or wait.

### Think before reading

Does a request need an extra word for be after the command to make it a complete sentence?

### Build the pattern

Useful respectful or plural commands include बसात, sit; येयात, come; दियात, give; and रावात, stay or wait. The command itself is finite and does not need आसा added after it. A place or object can be put before the command to specify what the listener should do.

म्हाका उदक दियात asks an adult to give water to me. Here म्हाका is a recipient, a concrete counterpart to the experiencer role in a need statement. Compare म्हाका उदक जाय with म्हाका उदक दियात: the first states a want, while the second requests an action.

हांगा रावात can mean stay here or wait here according to the situation. In a queue or meeting arrangement, waiting is the natural interpretation. A dictionary gloss alone cannot choose between every contextual meaning of a common verb; imagine the action and the setting.

Konkani politeness is not reducible to one word for please. Respectful verb forms, tone and the relationship between speakers all contribute. The practice uses respectful forms suitable for a first public exchange, while acknowledging that familiar commands appear naturally among friends. Do not switch to a different regional imperative paradigm simply because a phrase list uses another spelling. Keep the selected Goan forms together until you can deliberately recognise the variation.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Lesson 9: Present actions with a nasal first person

**Outcome:** Describe reading and working in the present.

### Think before reading

The third-person form is करता. Could first-person करतां differ through nasalisation rather than an entirely new word?

### Build the pattern

Konkani present action forms can be used for ordinary activities and, in context, what is happening now. तो वाचता means he reads or is reading. The first-person form is हांव वाचतां. The nasal ending matters, just as it mattered in the first-person existence form आसां.

करप is the dictionary-style verbal noun or infinitive label for do in many Konkani learning materials. In a sentence, करता or करतां supplies the appropriate present form. The ending -प does not itself make an ordinary finite statement. Learn the citation form and the sentence form as different grammatical roles.

काम करता expresses works or does work. An activity can be a noun-plus-verb combination rather than a one-word match for English. Objects precede the action: हांव पुस्तक वाचतां. The book is neuter, but that does not determine the first-person nasal ending in this present construction.

Unlike Marathi’s simple feminine जाते pattern, third-person Konkani present verbs in the examples remain the same with तो and ती: ती काम करता. This is a concrete place where neighbouring-language resemblance can mislead. Learn the actual Konkani contrast before transferring anything. A time word such as आयज, today, can clarify the frame, while the verb remains a present form. More precise temporal contrasts will develop through later past and future lessons.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| हांव पुस्तक वाचतां. | Hā̃v pustak vāchtā̃. | I read a book. |
| तो पुस्तक वाचता. | To pustak vāchtā. | He reads a book. |
| ती काम करता. | Tī kām kartā. | She works. |
| हांव काम करतां. | Hā̃v kām kartā̃. | I work. |
| तो काम करता. | To kām kartā. | He works. |
| ती पुस्तक वाचता. | Tī pustak vāchtā. | She reads a book. |

### Common trap

Do not replace ती करता with a Marathi feminine करते. In the selected Konkani present pattern, third-person singular gender does not produce that contrast. First-person nasalisation is a different grammatical distinction.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Say you read a book.
2. Say he reads a book.
3. Say she works.
4. Say you work.

***Answers:***

1. `हांव पुस्तक वाचतां.` - `Hā̃v pustak vāchtā̃.`
2. `तो पुस्तक वाचता.` - `To pustak vāchtā.`
3. `ती काम करता.` - `Tī kām kartā.`
4. `हांव काम करतां.` - `Hā̃v kām kartā̃.`

### Why these answers work

वाचतां and करतां identify first person through their nasal ending. The third-person forms वाचता and करता remain nonnasal here. पुस्तक is an object, while काम participates in the do-work expression. Neither replaces the subject as the person controller.

### Retrieve and recombine

Describe your reading, then describe a woman’s reading. Identify the ending that changes when the speaker becomes a third-person referent. Keep the book and action vocabulary constant.


## Lesson 10: Food objects and ordinary eating

**Outcome:** Describe eating and drinking with small, reusable vocabulary.

### Think before reading

If he eats a mango and she eats a mango, does the mango force the present verb to change?

### Build the pattern

खाता is a useful third-person present of eat, while खातां is first person in the chosen pattern. पितां and पिता provide corresponding first- and third-person drinking forms. Put the food or drink before the verb, keeping the person before the object in the neutral examples.

आंबो is a masculine mango; केळें is a neuter banana; उदक is water; and दूद is milk. These nouns will matter for descriptions and some completed-event constructions, but they do not determine the first-person versus third-person ending in the current present pattern.

जेवता means eats a meal or dines. It is useful to distinguish this from naming a particular food with खाता. Someone can answer a question about whether a person is having a meal without listing its contents. A language’s everyday meal vocabulary does not always divide activities exactly as English eat does.

The examples keep bare singular or mass objects when quantity is not the point. A bare food noun does not necessarily mean exactly one serving in every context. Later counting phrases will make quantities explicit. For now, focus on who performs the action and what is consumed. You can add here or today where it helps, but keep the core person-object-verb relation clear before making a longer sentence.

## Lesson 11: Negative habits are not simple absence

**Outcome:** Say that someone does not eat or work.

### Think before reading

Could adding a separate ना after every positive action always produce a correct negative verb?

### Build the pattern

Konkani action negatives often have their own forms. खायना means does not eat in the simple pattern here, while करना means does not do. These are not made by placing the existence negative ना after an unchanged खाता or करता. Learn the negative alongside its positive action.

हांव मास खायना means I do not eat meat. This can report a dietary habit rather than merely declining one serving now. मास is meat; नुस्तें is fish in the selected vocabulary. A speaker may distinguish these categories in food conversations, so do not assume an English dietary label carries the same practical scope without clarification.

तो काम करना says he does not work. This reports an activity’s absence, not that the person is physically absent. Compare तो हांगा ना: he is not here. The two negative messages can both be true, but they answer different questions.

The current examples stay with a small set of established present negatives instead of pretending that one spelling operation conjugates every Konkani verb. Negative forms can involve changes in the stem and depend on person or construction. When learning a new action, ask for both a positive and a negative sentence in the same variety. That provides useful evidence for the pattern rather than encouraging unsupported analogy. A precise negative can prevent confusion at a meal or when assigning work.

| ती काम करना. | Tī kām karnā. | She does not work. |

| ती नुस्तें खायना. | Tī nustẽ khāynā. | She does not eat fish. |

Do not treat physical absence and a negative habit as the same construction. Also, a statement about not eating meat may need a separate fish clarification in a real meal discussion rather than an assumed cultural equivalence.

## Lesson 12: My house, my bottle and my road

**Outcome:** Match a possessive to the related noun.

### Think before reading

A man owns a bottle and a house. Does the word my follow the man or the two different nouns?

### Build the pattern

म्हजो, म्हजी and म्हजें are masculine, feminine and neuter singular forms of my. The ending follows the noun possessed or related, not the owner’s gender. म्हजें घर is my house and म्हजी बाटली is my bottle, regardless of who speaks.

तुजो, तुजी and तुजें give familiar your. तुमचो, तुमची and तुमचें give respectful or plural your. You already met the neuter name phrase. This lesson reveals that it belongs to a broader agreement family rather than being an isolated greeting formula.

The possessive precedes the noun: म्हजें पुस्तक. In हें म्हजें पुस्तक, this is my book, the short identity phrase can be used without a copula. Adding a location gives म्हजें पुस्तक हांगा आसा. The book is the thing being located; the speaker is only the person to whom it relates.

Possessive grammar also introduces relationships such as म्हजो इश्ट, my male friend, and does not imply literal ownership of a person. Learn the expression as a relation between two referents. If you replace the noun, check its gender before reusing the possessive ending. Keeping one owner constant while changing house, bottle and friend makes the correct controller easy to see.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| हें म्हजें घर. | Hẽ mhajẽ ghar. | This is my house. |
| ही म्हजी बाटली. | Hī mhajī bāṭlī. | This is my bottle. |
| हो म्हजो इश्ट. | Ho mhajo iśṭ. | This is my male friend. |
| तुमचें पुस्तक हांगा आसा. | Tumchẽ pustak hā̃gā āsā. | Your book is here, respectfully. |

### Common trap

Do not make म्हजी masculine because a man is speaking. The bottle controls the possessive ending. Also, the short identity expressions are deliberate Konkani constructions, not incomplete English sentences awaiting an imported is.

## Lesson 13: Inside a house and on a table

**Outcome:** Use noun-based location expressions.

### Think before reading

You can say here with हांगा. Does naming a house alone tell the listener that something is inside it?

### Build the pattern

घरांत means in the house, शाळेंत means in the school, and बाजारांत means in the market. The location information follows the noun and may change its shape. शाळा becomes शाळेंत rather than following the exact visible pattern of घरांत.

मेजार means on the table. It uses a different locative relationship from in the house. The noun मेज is table, and its location form packages the English on relationship into the word. Learning actual forms prevents the mistaken belief that every English preposition becomes one independent Konkani word.

पुस्तक मेजार आसा places the book on the table. The same location form can accompany different things, while the singular existence verb remains आसा. To ask where the book is, return to खंय rather than stacking a noun ending onto that question word.

घरांत and घरा should also be kept distinct. The latter will appear in homeward movement expressions, while the former explicitly locates something inside the house. Spoken uses can overlap in context, but a beginner should first understand the relationship each taught sentence expresses. Imagine the spatial scene before choosing the form: an object on a surface, a person inside a building, or movement home. This gives the suffix a meaning rather than reducing it to a string of letters to memorise.

| --- | --- | --- |

| तो शाळेंत आसा. | To śāḷẽt āsā. | He is in the school. |

## Lesson 14: One thing and several things

**Outcome:** Recognise noun-class plurals and plural existence.

### Think before reading

If one book is पुस्तक, would several books necessarily take the same plural ending as several bottles?

### Build the pattern

Konkani plural formation depends on noun class. पुस्तक gives पुस्तकां, books, while बाटली gives बाटल्यो, bottles. Masculine आंबो gives आंबे, mangoes. These are distinct patterns; one universal English-style plural suffix would obscure them.

Plural existence uses आसात: पुस्तकां हांगा आसात, the books are here. The singular counterpart has पुस्तक and आसा. The change therefore affects both the noun and the predicate. A plural thing is not converted into first person simply because English translates both as are.

Neuter plural reference can use तीं, while feminine plural reference commonly uses त्यो in the written forms selected here. Thus after mentioning bottles, त्यो थंय आसात can refer back to them. This lesson makes the noun explicit in practice because identifying the class is more important than memorising a pronoun detached from context.

घर also gives घरां, houses, and फूल gives फुलां, flowers. Notice the shared neuter pattern while resisting the temptation to predict every unfamiliar noun from its ending alone. Some nouns are unchanged or behave differently. Learn one ordinary plural with each new count noun. The practical aim is to distinguish a single missing object from a group and to ask for quantities in the next lesson with the appropriate noun already available.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| पुस्तकां हांगा आसात. | Pustakā̃ hā̃gā āsāt. | The books are here. |
| बाटल्यो थंय आसात. | Bāṭlyo thãy āsāt. | The bottles are there. |
| आंबे हांगा आसात. | Ā̃be hā̃gā āsāt. | The mangoes are here. |
| घरां थंय आसात. | Gharā̃ thãy āsāt. | The houses are there. |
| फुलां हांगा आसात. | Phulā̃ hā̃gā āsāt. | The flowers are here. |
| पुस्तक हांगा आसा. | Pustak hā̃gā āsā. | The book is here. |

Do not keep singular आसा when the explicit subject is plural in these existence statements. Also, the bottle plural बाटल्यो is not formed by copying the book plural’s ending.

2. Say the bottles are there.
3. Say the mangoes are here.

### Why these answers work

Each answer uses the noun’s plural form and आसात. The differences between पुस्तकां, बाटल्यो and आंबे reflect noun classes. The location adverbs do not change with number, so they provide a stable middle part of the sentence.

### Retrieve and recombine

Turn a singular book location into a plural one, then do the same with a bottle. Name both pieces that change and the place expression that remains constant.


## Lesson 15: Counting a useful quantity

**Outcome:** Request small numbers of books, bottles or mangoes.

### Think before reading

After learning plural nouns, can you insert a number before them while keeping the respectful request verb?

### Build the pattern

Useful small numbers are एक, one; दोन, two; तीन, three; चार, four; पांच, five; and स, six. The number precedes the counted noun. दोन पुस्तकां means two books and तीन बाटल्यो means three bottles.

A request adds दियात: दोन पुस्तकां दियात. The command is addressed to the listener and therefore does not change when the number of requested objects changes. This differs from a statement that plural objects exist, which takes आसात because the objects are its subject.

कितलीं can ask how many with neuter plural nouns, as कितलीं पुस्तकां आसात? The question form participates in noun agreement. Other noun classes require their own forms, so do not apply this neuter question unchanged to every object you can count. The practice keeps the question with books while using other nouns in numbered requests.

Counting phrases become useful when preparing materials, buying fruit or distributing bottles. A bare plural says there is more than one but does not provide a precise count. Conversely, a number without a clear noun can leave the listener unsure what to supply. Build the whole quantity phrase first, then decide whether you are requesting it or reporting its presence. The same phrase can enter either construction, with different final verbs.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |

| दोन पुस्तकां दियात. | Don pustakā̃ diyāt. | Give two books, respectfully. |

| चार आंबे दियात. | Chār ā̃be diyāt. | Give four mangoes, respectfully. |
| कितलीं पुस्तकां आसात? | Kitlī̃ pustakā̃ āsāt? | How many books are there? |

| पांच फुलां. | Pā̃ch phulā̃. | Five flowers. |

### Common trap

Do not use a plural existence verb in place of a request merely because the objects are plural. Also, कितलीं is the neuter plural question form here, not an unchanging translation of every how many.

2. Request three bottles respectfully.
3. Request four mangoes respectfully.

## Lesson 16: Today, tomorrow and a scheduled visit

**Outcome:** Place an action in time without confusing day words.

### Think before reading

Would knowing where someone comes tell you when they will arrive?

### Build the pattern

आयज means today, फाल्यां means tomorrow and काल means yesterday in the ordinary uses taught here. These are Konkani forms, not Marathi’s आज and उद्या. Similar concepts do not guarantee identical vocabulary in neighbouring languages.

केन्ना asks when. The question word targets time, while खंय targets place. A visitor can understand every other word and still answer the wrong question if this distinction is missed. Practise the two gaps separately: the place where someone is, and the time when they come.

An appointment hour can use वरांचेर, at an hour: तीन वरांचेर, at three o’clock. फाल्यां तीन वरांचेर येयात asks someone to come tomorrow at three. It is a respectful command scheduled in the future, not a full future indicative conjugation. You can arrange a visit before learning every future ending.

आतां means now. It can clarify an immediate request such as आतां हांगा रावात, wait here now. A time word does not itself make a malformed predicate grammatical, but it helps identify the intended event. Keep the action and its schedule distinct: what should happen, who should do it, and when. This gives you a compact but precise appointment message that can be changed one detail at a time.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| फाल्यां तीन वरांचेर येयात. | Phālyā̃ tīn varā̃cher yeyāt. | Come tomorrow at three, respectfully. |
| आयज हांगा रावात. | Āyj hā̃gā rāvāt. | Wait here today, respectfully. |
| आतां हांगा बसात. | Ātā̃ hā̃gā basāt. | Sit here now, respectfully. |
| फाल्यां चार वरांचेर येयात. | Phālyā̃ chār varā̃cher yeyāt. | Come tomorrow at four, respectfully. |
| काल. | Kāl. | Yesterday. |
| केन्ना? | Kennā? | When? |

### Common trap

Do not substitute Marathi day words because the scripts look familiar. Also, a place answer does not resolve केन्ना. An appointment needs the intended time, not merely a known location.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Ask someone to come tomorrow at three.
2. Ask someone to wait here today.
3. Ask someone to sit here now.
4. Ask someone to come tomorrow at four.

***Answers:***

1. `फाल्यां तीन वरांचेर येयात.` - `Phālyā̃ tīn varā̃cher yeyāt.`
2. `आयज हांगा रावात.` - `Āyj hā̃gā rāvāt.`
3. `आतां हांगा बसात.` - `Ātā̃ hā̃gā basāt.`
4. `फाल्यां चार वरांचेर येयात.` - `Phālyā̃ chār varā̃cher yeyāt.`

### Why these answers work

फाल्यां and आयज distinguish days, while आतां marks the immediate moment. वरांचेर packages an hour as an appointment time. The respectful commands remain unchanged when the schedule shifts from three to four.

### Retrieve and recombine

Change the visit from tomorrow at three to today at four. Say the new message in one sentence, then retrieve the separate questions for when and where.


## Lesson 17: Movement toward a destination

**Outcome:** Say where someone goes and distinguish come from go.

### Think before reading

When a friend moves toward the place where you are waiting, does come express a different viewpoint from go?

### Build the pattern

वचप is the common citation form for go, with present वता for third-person singular and वतां for first person in the selected Goan pattern. येवप means come, with येता and येतां in the corresponding present forms. The viewpoint distinction resembles English come and go but is determined by the actual conversation.

घरा appears in homeward expressions: हांव घरा वतां, I go home. शाळेंत can accompany movement to school, as तो शाळेंत वता. The same location-looking form can be used with a motion predicate, but that does not mean all place expressions are interchangeable.

बाजारांत places the movement in or toward the market in the practical sentence ती बाजारांत वता. The action verb clarifies that the subject is going rather than simply being there. Compare ती बाजारांत आसा to see the difference between a location state and a trip.

Do not derive every movement form by adding a predictable suffix to the dictionary entry. वचप and वता already show a stem difference. Learn the common finite pair with its meaning. A small set of verified motion sentences is enough to describe a route from home to school or a friend’s arrival here, while later lessons introduce completed motion and a future visit. Track time perspective separately from the chosen destination.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| हांव घरा वतां. | Hā̃v gharā vatā̃. | I go home. |
| तो शाळेंत वता. | To śāḷẽt vatā. | He goes to school. |
| ती बाजारांत वता. | Tī bājārā̃t vatā. | She goes to the market. |
| ती हांगा येता. | Tī hā̃gā yetā. | She comes here. |
| हांव हांगा येतां. | Hā̃v hā̃gā yetā̃. | I come here. |
| ती बाजारांत आसा. | Tī bājārā̃t āsā. | She is in the market. |

### Common trap

Do not confuse a place state with movement just because the location phrase is identical. Also, वचप and वता are related forms of go, not evidence that every verb can be conjugated by deleting its last letter.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Say you go home.
2. Say he goes to school.
3. Say she goes to the market.
4. Say she comes here.

***Answers:***

1. `हांव घरा वतां.` - `Hā̃v gharā vatā̃.`
2. `तो शाळेंत वता.` - `To śāḷẽt vatā.`
3. `ती बाजारांत वता.` - `Tī bājārā̃t vatā.`
4. `ती हांगा येता.` - `Tī hā̃gā yetā.`

### Why these answers work

वतां marks first person while वता marks third person in these present motion examples. येता changes the movement viewpoint to coming. The final place phrase remains interpretable through its combination with the motion or existence predicate.

### Retrieve and recombine

Describe a friend at the market, then going to the market, as separate scenes. Finally imagine the friend approaching you and choose the coming verb rather than automatically repeating go.


## Lesson 18: A completed arrival and departure

**Outcome:** Report where a man or adult woman went.

### Think before reading

Does the present वता reveal the irregular stem needed for a completed went?

### Build the pattern

Completed motion uses forms different from the present. तो घरा गेलो means he went home, while ती घरा गेली means an adult woman went home in the reference pattern used here. The past stem गेल- must be learned; it is not obtained by adding an English-like past ending to वता.

For come, आयलो and आयली provide masculine and feminine completed forms. A report that someone came here is different from saying the person is here now. Arrival can be complete even if the person later left. Keep the event and the current location as separate facts unless the context connects them.

Konkani agreement for female referents can reflect age, familiarity and dialect in ways not captured by a simple biological-sex rule. The course labels its adult feminine examples and does not teach one form as universal for every girl or woman. If a local speaker uses a neuter reference pattern, investigate the social and dialectal context rather than dismissing it.

काल can locate the event yesterday, but the completed verb already contributes an event perspective. A short narrative may establish the day once and then continue with several completed actions. The current lesson stays with intransitive movement, whose agreement should not be transferred automatically to completed actions with an object. That additional contrast is introduced separately.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| तो घरा गेलो. | To gharā gelo. | He went home. |
| ती घरा गेली. | Tī gharā gelī. | She went home, adult feminine reference. |
| तो हांगा आयलो. | To hā̃gā āylo. | He came here. |
| ती हांगा आयली. | Tī hā̃gā āylī. | She came here, adult feminine reference. |
| तो काल शाळेंत गेलो. | To kāl śāḷẽt gelo. | He went to school yesterday. |
| ती काल बाजारांत गेली. | Tī kāl bājārā̃t gelī. | She went to the market yesterday. |

### Common trap

Do not make went by extending the present वता. Also, a completed arrival does not guarantee current presence. Treat gender and social-reference patterns as part of the selected variety, not as a universal rule for all Konkani speakers.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Say he went home.
2. Say an adult woman went home, using ती.
3. Say he came here.
4. Say an adult woman came here, using ती.

***Answers:***

1. `तो घरा गेलो.` - `To gharā gelo.`
2. `ती घरा गेली.` - `Tī gharā gelī.`
3. `तो हांगा आयलो.` - `To hā̃gā āylo.`
4. `ती हांगा आयली.` - `Tī hā̃gā āylī.`

### Why these answers work

गेलो and गेली report completed going; आयलो and आयली report completed coming. The location expressions remain unchanged while the adult masculine or feminine reference changes the past form in these examples.

### Retrieve and recombine

Tell a two-event story in which a man came here and later went home. Then retell it with the explicitly taught adult feminine reference and compare the two changed predicates.


## Lesson 19: A future visitor

**Outcome:** Describe an expected visit with a small future pattern.

### Think before reading

If he comes now is येता, what does the extra material in येतलो contribute to a tomorrow statement?

### Build the pattern

येतलो expresses a future coming event with a masculine singular referent in the pattern here. तो फाल्यां येतलो means he will come tomorrow. Adult feminine ती takes येतली in the selected written reference pattern. The future is not formed by adding आसा after an unchanged present येता.

For go, वतलो and वतली provide corresponding masculine and adult feminine future forms. These are useful for a visitor’s next destination. The course keeps third-person singular futures first because Konkani future endings also interact with person and reference conventions. One reliable contrast is better than a guessed full table.

फाल्यां makes the intended time explicit. It is possible to discuss future events with other time expressions, but a clear tomorrow sentence helps separate the new future form from the past गेलो and present वता. The same destination can therefore appear in three time perspectives.

A future statement can express an expectation or a plan depending on the speaker’s information. It is not automatically a promise made by the person being discussed. If you tell someone that a friend will come, ensure that the statement reflects what you actually know. In language practice, make the imagined situation explicit: a confirmed plan, a prediction or an invitation. The grammar supplies the future frame; the conversation supplies the commitment.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| तो फाल्यां येतलो. | To phālyā̃ yetlo. | He will come tomorrow. |
| ती फाल्यां येतली. | Tī phālyā̃ yetlī. | She will come tomorrow, adult feminine reference. |
| तो घरा वतलो. | To gharā vatlo. | He will go home. |
| ती बाजारांत वतली. | Tī bājārā̃t vatlī. | She will go to the market, adult feminine reference. |

| तो फाल्यां येतलो? | To phālyā̃ yetlo? | Will he come tomorrow? |

### Common trap

Do not use the same future form for every person based only on these third-person examples. Also, gender-reference conventions vary, so the adult feminine form is a labelled course choice rather than a claim about every social setting.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

2. Say an adult woman will come tomorrow.
3. Say he will go home.
4. Say an adult woman will go to the market.

***Answers:***

1. `तो फाल्यां येतलो.` - `To phālyā̃ yetlo.`

4. `ती बाजारांत वतली.` - `Tī bājārā̃t vatlī.`

### Why these answers work

येतलो and येतली express future coming; वतलो and वतली future going. The third-person reference determines the selected endings. Time and destination phrases remain before the future predicate, which does not need a separate positive copula.

### Retrieve and recombine

Give a friend’s present location, yesterday’s departure and tomorrow’s visit as three separate sentences. Keep the person fixed and retrieve a distinct predicate for each time perspective.


## Lesson 20: Completed reading with an agent form

**Outcome:** Recognise that a completed transitive action can follow its object.

### Think before reading

If I read a neuter book, might the past form reflect the book instead of the reader?

### Build the pattern

हांवें पुस्तक वाचलें means I read a book as a completed event. हांवें is an agent form used in this completed transitive construction, different from ordinary हांव in the present. The completed वाचलें is neuter singular with पुस्तक.

ताणें पुस्तक वाचलें identifies a male agent in the same pattern. The object remains the same, so the completed verb remains neuter. This contrast prevents a misleading universal instruction to make every verb agree with the person doing the action.

With a masculine mango as the object, a completed eating example is हांवें आंबो खाल्लो. The noun’s gender becomes visible in the completed predicate. Actual Konkani perfective systems vary by dialect and construction, and object marking can affect agreement. This lesson deliberately stays with a few unmarked singular objects rather than extrapolating to every transitive verb.

Compare the completed reading sentence with हांव पुस्तक वाचतां. The ordinary present uses a first-person nasal ending, while the completed event changes the agent form and the verb. The distinction involves more than replacing an English tense word. A useful learner can recognise which event perspective is being presented and which noun controls the visible agreement. Keep these complete pairs available when retelling a small reading or meal event.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| हांवें पुस्तक वाचलें. | Hā̃vẽ pustak vāchlẽ. | I read a book as a completed event. |
| ताणें पुस्तक वाचलें. | Tāṇẽ pustak vāchlẽ. | He read a book as a completed event. |
| हांवें आंबो खाल्लो. | Hā̃vẽ ā̃bo khāllo. | I ate a mango. |
| ताणें आंबो खाल्लो. | Tāṇẽ ā̃bo khāllo. | He ate a mango. |
| हांव पुस्तक वाचतां. | Hā̃v pustak vāchtā̃. | I read a book in the present. |
| तो आंबो खाता. | To ā̃bo khātā. | He eats a mango in the present. |

### Common trap

Do not replace हांवें with ordinary हांव inside the completed transitive pattern just because English says I. Also, the completed object’s gender and the present subject’s person are different agreement controls.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Say you read a book as a completed event.
2. Say he read a book as a completed event.
3. Say you ate a mango.
4. Say he ate a mango.

***Answers:***

1. `हांवें पुस्तक वाचलें.` - `Hā̃vẽ pustak vāchlẽ.`

3. `हांवें आंबो खाल्लो.` - `Hā̃vẽ ā̃bo khāllo.`
4. `ताणें आंबो खाल्लो.` - `Tāṇẽ ā̃bo khāllo.`

### Why these answers work

The book answers share neuter वाचलें; the mango answers share masculine खाल्लो. Changing the agent from हांवें to ताणें does not change the object, so it does not change these completed verb forms.

### Retrieve and recombine

Retell a present reading activity as a completed event. Identify both the agent-form change and the verb change. Then swap the agent while keeping the book constant.


## Lesson 21: A want to perform an action

**Outcome:** Express wanting to eat, go or read with an infinitival form.

### Think before reading

You can want a book. If you instead want to read, what form lets an action occupy the wanted slot?

### Build the pattern

The want construction can take an infinitival action. म्हाका वाचूंक जाय means I want to read. The experiencer remains म्हाका and जाय retains the want meaning, while वाचूंक names the desired action. This is different from a finite present statement that I read.

वचूंक means to go, खावंक to eat, and करूंक to do in the forms selected here. The visible endings are not identical after every verb stem, so learn the useful infinitives directly. The citation form in -प and the infinitive used in this construction are related but do not serve exactly the same syntactic role.

An object can appear before the infinitive: म्हाका पुस्तक वाचूंक जाय, I want to read a book. The same book that was wanted as an item can now be the object of a wanted action. This expands a small vocabulary into a more precise intention.

A familiar question तुका कितें खावंक जाय? asks what you want to eat. Respectful तुमकां can replace तुका without changing the action’s object slot. The question is about desire, not ability or permission. Someone may want to eat something that is unavailable, or want to go somewhere they cannot yet reach. Keep these meanings separate rather than treating every English want to or can phrase as the same modal construction.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| म्हाका वाचूंक जाय. | Mhākā vāchū̃k jāy. | I want to read. |
| म्हाका घरा वचूंक जाय. | Mhākā gharā vachū̃k jāy. | I want to go home. |
| म्हाका पुस्तक वाचूंक जाय. | Mhākā pustak vāchū̃k jāy. | I want to read a book. |
| तुमकां कितें खावंक जाय? | Tumkā̃ kitẽ khāvãk jāy? | What do you want to eat, respectfully? |
| म्हाका काम करूंक जाय. | Mhākā kām karū̃k jāy. | I want to work. |
| तुका कितें खावंक जाय? | Tukā kitẽ khāvãk jāy? | What do you want to eat, familiarly? |

### Common trap

Do not place a complete finite present वाचतां in the infinitive slot. Also, wanting does not establish ability or permission. The infinitive names the desired action while जाय supplies the want relation.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Say you want to read.
2. Say you want to go home.
3. Say you want to read a book.
4. Ask an adult what they want to eat.

***Answers:***

1. `म्हाका वाचूंक जाय.` - `Mhākā vāchū̃k jāy.`
2. `म्हाका घरा वचूंक जाय.` - `Mhākā gharā vachū̃k jāy.`
3. `म्हाका पुस्तक वाचूंक जाय.` - `Mhākā pustak vāchū̃k jāy.`
4. `तुमकां कितें खावंक जाय?` - `Tumkā̃ kitẽ khāvãk jāy?`

### Why these answers work

The first three retain म्हाका and replace the wanted item with an infinitival action phrase. The fourth uses respectful तुमकां and asks for the food object with कितें. The infinitive remains nonfinite rather than carrying present subject agreement.

### Retrieve and recombine

Turn I want a book into I want to read a book. Explain the new role of the book and the added action form. Then transfer the pattern to going home.


## Lesson 22: Joining facts and contrasting them

**Outcome:** Connect actions or descriptions with and and but.

### Think before reading

If a woman reads and a man works, should a conjunction erase either clause’s own predicate?

### Build the pattern

आनी means and and connects words, phrases or clauses. ती वाचता आनी तो काम करता adds one fact to another. Each clause remains a complete present statement. The conjunction does not force a new plural agreement on the two separate predicates.

पूण means but in the standard written form used here. तो हांगा आसा पूण ती थंय आसा contrasts two locations. The two statements might both be true; but tells the listener to notice their contrast rather than merely adding them.

वा can offer an alternative, while a short choice often uses काय in expressions such as उदक काय दूद? Context makes this a choice between drinks rather than a request for an object’s identity. The course uses the complete short expression and does not claim that every English or has one fixed Konkani equivalent.

Connected language becomes easier when you first build each part independently. Check its subject, object or location, and predicate. Then choose the connector that explains the relationship. If the connector is removed, the underlying facts should still be understandable. This method prevents long sentences from becoming a string of familiar words without clear roles. It also lets you repair one part of the message without starting the entire conversation again.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ती वाचता आनी तो काम करता. | Tī vāchtā ānī to kām kartā. | She reads and he works. |
| तो हांगा आसा पूण ती थंय आसा. | To hā̃gā āsā pūṇ tī thãy āsā. | He is here but she is there. |
| पुस्तक आनी बाटली. | Pustak ānī bāṭlī. | A book and a bottle. |
| उदक काय दूद? | Udak kāy dūd? | Water or milk, as a choice? |
| आयज वा फाल्यां. | Āyj vā phālyā̃. | Today or tomorrow. |
| हांव वाचतां आनी तो जेवता. | Hā̃v vāchtā̃ ānī to jevtā. | I read and he eats a meal. |

### Common trap

Do not treat आनी and पूण as interchangeable decorations. They communicate different relationships between facts. Also, the choice expression’s काय should be understood through the whole exchange rather than assigned a single English meaning everywhere.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Say she reads and he works.
2. Say he is here but she is there.
3. Name a book and a bottle together.
4. Offer the choice water or milk.

***Answers:***

1. `ती वाचता आनी तो काम करता.` - `Tī vāchtā ānī to kām kartā.`
2. `तो हांगा आसा पूण ती थंय आसा.` - `To hā̃gā āsā pūṇ tī thãy āsā.`
3. `पुस्तक आनी बाटली.` - `Pustak ānī bāṭlī.`
4. `उदक काय दूद?` - `Udak kāy dūd?`

### Why these answers work

आनी adds two activities or noun phrases. पूण contrasts the two locations while retaining each copula. The short drink choice is an elliptical conversational turn, so it does not need to be forced into a full existence statement.

### Retrieve and recombine

Connect a reading activity and a meal, then contrast two people’s locations. Identify what relationship the connector adds beyond the two facts themselves.


## Lesson 23: A market exchange with clear quantities

**Outcome:** Check availability and request the quantity you actually want.

### Think before reading

Knowing a seller has fruit does not establish whether mangoes are available. How can you check the particular item?

### Build the pattern

A market conversation first needs a clear item and a check that it is available. आंबे आसात? asks whether there are mangoes. The plural noun pairs with आसात, and question intonation turns the statement into a check. You do not need to import an English do or any neighbouring language’s question particle.

This availability question differs from कितलीं पुस्तकां आसात, how many books are there. One checks whether the requested kind of item is present; the other asks for a count. A positive stock answer still leaves quantity and price unresolved. Treat the response as information about availability rather than as confirmation of every detail of a purchase.

Once the item and price are understood, request a quantity: दोन आंबे दियात. A need statement such as म्हाका उदक जाय can open a search for a drink, while म्हाका उदक दियात requests the giving action. If water is unavailable, उदक ना supplies an absence statement rather than a personal refusal.

A short market exchange can therefore move through identifying the object, checking availability and specifying quantity. Keep these turns separable so they transfer to another stall. You should also recognise the limits of a small number vocabulary: asking a price does not guarantee understanding every possible answer. Request repetition, point to a written amount where appropriate, and check the quantity you intend. The linguistic goal is a clear shared transaction rather than reciting a phrase without understanding its reply.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| आंबे आसात? | Ā̃be āsāt? | Are there mangoes available? |
| दोन आंबे दियात. | Don ā̃be diyāt. | Give two mangoes, respectfully. |
| म्हाका उदक जाय. | Mhākā udak jāy. | I want water. |
| उदक ना. | Udak nā. | There is no water. |
| तीन पुस्तकां दियात. | Tīn pustakā̃ diyāt. | Give three books, respectfully. |
| म्हाका उदक दियात. | Mhākā udak diyāt. | Give me water, respectfully. |

### Common trap

An availability question does not establish the price or reserve a quantity. Also, a need statement and a giving request perform different actions in the exchange, even when they mention the same water.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Ask whether mangoes are available.
2. Request two mangoes respectfully.
3. Say you want water.
4. Say there is no water.

***Answers:***

1. `आंबे आसात?` - `Ā̃be āsāt?`
2. `दोन आंबे दियात.` - `Don ā̃be diyāt.`
3. `म्हाका उदक जाय.` - `Mhākā udak jāy.`
4. `उदक ना.` - `Udak nā.`

### Why these answers work

The first checks a plural item’s availability with आसात. The second puts a number and plural noun before दियात. The third states a need with म्हाका and जाय. The fourth denies availability with ना, without inventing a positive copula.

### Retrieve and recombine

Move the exchange from fruit to books. Request three books, check mango availability and state a need for water separately. Explain which turn asks for information and which asks for an action.


## Lesson 24: Repairing a visit arrangement

**Outcome:** Ask for slower speech, repetition and a clear appointment.

### Think before reading

If you heard a time too quickly, do you need a new plan or a clearer repetition of the same plan?

### Build the pattern

सावकास उलयात asks someone to speak slowly. उलयप is a common Goan verb for speaking; it should not be silently replaced by a neighbouring language’s everyday speech verb. परत सांगात asks the listener to say or tell it again. The two requests address different problems: speed and missing content.

A visit arrangement can be rebuilt from known pieces. फाल्यां तीन वरांचेर येयात schedules a respectful request to come. हांगा रावात asks the person to wait here. If the visitor’s future arrival is being reported to someone else, तो फाल्यां येतलो uses a future statement rather than an imperative.

Notice the change in participant roles. The command addresses the visitor, while the future statement talks about him. Even when the same real person is involved, the sentence chooses a different pronoun and predicate because the speech situation has changed.

Conversation repair is productive language use. Ask for repetition of the detail you missed rather than pretending the entire message is clear. If the day is understood but the hour is uncertain, focus on the hour. If the action itself was unclear, request the whole sentence again. The course’s practice uses complete short turns so they remain useful outside a memorised dialogue. A successful outcome is a shared understanding of who comes, where they wait and when the meeting happens.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| सावकास उलयात. | Sāvakās ulayāt. | Speak slowly, respectfully. |
| परत सांगात. | Parat sā̃gāt. | Say it again, respectfully. |
| फाल्यां तीन वरांचेर येयात. | Phālyā̃ tīn varā̃cher yeyāt. | Come tomorrow at three, respectfully. |
| हांगा रावात. | Hā̃gā rāvāt. | Wait here, respectfully. |
| तो फाल्यां येतलो. | To phālyā̃ yetlo. | He will come tomorrow. |
| तुमी खंय आसात? | Tumī khãy āsāt? | Where are you, respectfully? |

### Common trap

Do not use the future report येतलो as though it directly commands the listener to come. Also, slower speech and repeated content are separate requests; choose the repair that addresses the actual gap.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Ask someone to speak slowly.
2. Ask someone to say it again.
3. Ask someone to come tomorrow at three.
4. Ask someone to wait here.

***Answers:***

1. `सावकास उलयात.` - `Sāvakās ulayāt.`
2. `परत सांगात.` - `Parat sā̃gāt.`
3. `फाल्यां तीन वरांचेर येयात.` - `Phālyā̃ tīn varā̃cher yeyāt.`
4. `हांगा रावात.` - `Hā̃gā rāvāt.`

### Why these answers work

उलयात and सांगात are respectful commands for speaking and telling. The appointment retains येयात, while waiting uses रावात. The time and location phrases specify circumstances without changing which action is requested.

### Retrieve and recombine

Role-play a three-turn visit arrangement, including one repair request. Then tell a third person about the visitor’s future arrival and switch from direct address to a report.


## Lesson 25: A coherent account with several patterns

**Outcome:** Recombine a present location, a completed event, a need and a future visit.

### Think before reading

Does English I always map to हांव when you describe being here, reading a completed book and wanting water?

### Build the pattern

The final review brings different grammatical relationships into one small account. Ordinary हांव works as the subject of present location or activity. हांवें marks the agent in the completed transitive examples. म्हाका identifies the experiencer of a need. These forms are not interchangeable ways to sound more formal; they belong to different constructions.

A coherent story can say that you are here, that you read a book earlier, that you now want water, and that a friend will come tomorrow. The vocabulary is modest, but the predicates express distinct time perspectives and participant roles. The completed book event uses neuter वाचलें; the future visitor uses masculine येतलो in the explicitly selected reference pattern.

Before speaking, identify what the sentence is doing: locating, describing an activity, reporting a completed event, stating a need, asking a question or requesting an action. Then choose the relevant Konkani structure. Similar Devanagari spelling is not a licence to import Marathi grammar, and an English word-for-word order will not reliably supply the correct form.

Continue with audio and fluent-speaker review in the variety you intend to use. Konkani has substantial regional, community and script traditions, and this course’s Goan written standard is one stated choice. The romanisation cannot represent every sound or social nuance. A useful foundation lets you recombine known material, recognise a different construction and ask for clarification. It does not certify fluency or erase the need for feedback.

### Worked language

| Native script | Romanisation | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| हांव हांगा आसां. | Hā̃v hā̃gā āsā̃. | I am here. |
| हांवें पुस्तक वाचलें. | Hā̃vẽ pustak vāchlẽ. | I read a book as a completed event. |
| म्हाका उदक जाय. | Mhākā udak jāy. | I want water. |
| तो फाल्यां येतलो. | To phālyā̃ yetlo. | He will come tomorrow. |
| तुमचें पुस्तक मेजार आसा. | Tumchẽ pustak mejār āsā. | Your book is on the table, respectfully. |
| परत सांगात. | Parat sā̃gāt. | Say it again, respectfully. |

### Common trap

Do not collapse हांव, हांवें and म्हाका into a single first-person form. Also, the course’s labelled reference patterns are not claims that all Konkani communities use identical gender, tense or address forms.

### Practice

Build one complete sentence for each situation.

1. Say you are here.
2. Say you read a book as a completed event.
3. Say you want water.
4. Say he will come tomorrow.

***Answers:***

1. `हांव हांगा आसां.` - `Hā̃v hā̃gā āsā̃.`
2. `हांवें पुस्तक वाचलें.` - `Hā̃vẽ pustak vāchlẽ.`
3. `म्हाका उदक जाय.` - `Mhākā udak jāy.`
4. `तो फाल्यां येतलो.` - `To phālyā̃ yetlo.`

### Why these answers work

The location uses an ordinary first-person subject and आसां. Completed reading uses agent-marked हांवें and neuter वाचलें. Wanting uses म्हाका with जाय. The future visitor uses a third-person future form and an explicit tomorrow expression.

### Retrieve and recombine

Create six connected but short messages for a visit: an introduction, location, question, completed event, need and future report. Change the object once and inspect only the agreement that actually depends on it.
